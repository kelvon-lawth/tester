Derived user story (the ROADMAP goal line is not in user-story format; this story is derived from it and the phase success criteria, see return notes): **As a** local business owner (and the freelancer showing the prototype), **I want to** open the site by double-clicking it and immediately see one consistent bright, friendly, professional look, **so that** it reads as a real local marketer I can trust, and every later page builds on the same foundation.

</objective>
